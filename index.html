About Ecogas

Making lithium hydroxide for batteries leaves large amounts of sodium sulphate behind, as a solution, a brine or a solid. For most refiners it is a costly waste stream to store, transport and dispose of.

Ecogas is an Australian company focused on that problem. We work with lithium producers to find the best route for their Na₂SO₄: treatment, recovery, reuse or responsible disposal.

What we do

End-to-end support for sodium sulphate solutions, brines and solids.

01

By-product management

We collect, store and transport sodium sulphate solutions and solids from your site, safely and in line with regulations.

02

Treatment and recovery

We purify and concentrate sodium sulphate streams so they can be reused or sold instead of sent to landfill.

03

Reuse and offtake

We connect recovered sodium sulphate with buyers in detergents, glass, paper, textiles and construction materials.

04

Assessment and advice

We review your process and by-product data and recommend the most cost-effective, lowest-impact option.

Who we help

  • Lithium hydroxide refiners
  • Battery materials and precursor plants
  • Mining and mineral processing operations
  • Chemical manufacturers with sulphate waste streams
