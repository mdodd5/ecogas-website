The problem

Every tonne of battery lithium leaves something behind.

Refining spodumene into battery-grade lithium hydroxide produces large volumes of sodium sulphate as a solution, a brine or a solid.

For most refiners it is a costly stream to store, transport and dispose of, and much of it ends up in landfill. As lithium demand grows, so does the pile.

Ecogas exists to close that loop.
