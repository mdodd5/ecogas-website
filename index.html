The opportunity

Two industries, one missing link.

Lithium makes a waste problem. Refining lithium and recycling batteries produce large volumes of sodium sulphate. Most of it is stockpiled or sent to landfill at a cost.

Soda ash has an energy problem. Much of the world's soda ash is made by the Solvay process, which needs high-temperature limestone kilns and leaves calcium chloride as waste.

Ecogas connects the two, turning one industry's waste into another's raw material.

Our technology

Sulphate in. Soda ash and gypsum out.

A patent-pending conversion, running at or below 100 °C, that reacts sodium sulphate with common limestone (CaCO₃). It works on Na₂SO₄ from any source, as a solution, brine or solid.
